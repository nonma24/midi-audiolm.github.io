MIDI-AudioLM:Joint Multi-Track MIDI and Audio Modeling for Controllable Song Generation

One model writes multi-track MIDI and audio in a single interleaved stream — the score comes first and stays editable, the waveform follows it.

All samples below are raw model output, no post-processing. MIDI is rendered with FluidR3 GM for listening only. Click or drag anywhere on a lane to play from that point; each card remembers where you stopped.
